How the program works

Simple to run. Powerful results.

Your members take orders and collect payment upfront—then we bake, box, and deliver. There is nothing to pre-purchase, no inventory to manage, and no financial risk to your group. Customers come back every year asking when the next sale is.

No upfront costs

Your organization pays nothing in advance. Orders are collected, then we fulfill.

No minimum orders

Sell one cake or a thousand—there is no floor. Every sale counts toward your goal.

Fully guaranteed

If something is not right, we make it right—every time.
