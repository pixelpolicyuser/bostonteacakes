How the Program Works

Simple to Run.
Powerful Results.

A Boston Tea Cake Company fundraiser is one of the easiest campaigns your organization will ever run. Your members take orders and collect payment upfront — then we bake, box, and deliver. There's nothing to pre-purchase, no inventory to manage, and no financial risk to your group.

The product does the selling for you. Gourmet bundt cakes are a meaningful, memorable alternative to gift wrap, popcorn, or candy — and customers come back every year asking when the next sale is. Many of our fundraising partners have run annual campaigns with BTCC for years, even decades.

School & Allergen Safety

A Fundraiser Safe for Every Student

For school districts and youth organizations, allergen safety isn't optional — it's essential. The Boston Tea Cake Company operates a 100% nut-free bakery facility. No BTCC cake contains nuts of any kind, and all ingredients are kosher-certified.

This makes our fundraising program one of the few gourmet cake options truly safe to bring into school environments — no waivers, no workarounds, no exceptions needed. Every cake is baked in a facility where nuts are never present.

Our Lotsa Lemon cake takes allergen-friendliness even further — it is completely Dairy-Free and Soy-Free, containing only Wheat and Eggs. A delicious option for students and families with additional dietary restrictions.
