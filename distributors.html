The Product Line

Six Flavors.
All Exceptional.

Our signature line consists of six gourmet 8" bundt cakes, available in Small (1.5 lb, serves 6–8) and Large (3 lb, serves 12–16) sizes. Every cake is baked fresh from scratch in our Milford, Massachusetts facility and packaged in individual old-fashioned white bakery boxes — presentation-ready right out of the box.

Shelf life ranges from 10 to 21 days at room temperature depending on variety, and all cakes can be refrigerated or frozen for extended life — a practical advantage for wholesale and retail accounts alike.

Packaging

Each cake arrives in an individual old-fashioned white bakery box — no additional packaging required for retail display or gifting. Cakes may be refrigerated for longer shelf life, frozen and refrozen, and if sold frozen, used within 120 days of sell date.
